## Definitions and provenance

Onset is sample 161 (one-based); samples 1–160 are the normal prefix. Sampling period: 3 minutes. Warm-up excludes incomplete windows. Flags use the frozen PCA OR KS rule, with no post-test threshold tuning.

Equal-fault-weighted mean of detected-run latency means: 55.255 steps. Median of per-fault detected-run medians: 19.25 steps. 20/20 types had a detection.

Normal-prefix OR false-positive rate: 0.43%.

First flags in long fault runs can also be background false alarms; a first-flag detection does not establish attribution to the injected fault. Post-onset flag rates and per-component latencies are included for interpretation. Overlapping windows are dependent, and a window false-positive rate is not a probability of a false alarm per run.

The test fault condition labels identify whole runs, including their normal prefixes. The fault onset is the documented release convention, not a per-row label. Output-quality deterioration or lead time before quality loss is not measured by this benchmark.
